# 🎵 Forge — Donna AI Lyrics Format (Corrected)

Vanito taught us the key difference. Memorize this:

**`[Brackets]` = Architecture** (sections, voices)
**`(Parentheses)` = Acting** (delivery, tone)

## Quick Examples

```
[VERSE 1]                 ← bracket = section label
Normal lyrics here...
Anata no soba ni itai

[CHORUS]                  ← bracket = section label
Sakura no chikai! (yell)  ← parenthesis = delivery instruction
I will never let you down!
(scream) RISE UP AGAIN!   ← parenthesis = delivery instruction

[BRIDGE]
(whisper) I made it, Dad... I made it...
```

## Rule of Thumb

- `[ ]` = what part of the song / who sings it
- `( )` = how the line is performed

Don't save anything to vault/hub without Vanito's say-so.
