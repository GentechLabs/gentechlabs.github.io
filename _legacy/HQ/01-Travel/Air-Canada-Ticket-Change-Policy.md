# Air Canada Ticket Change Policy

**Your ticket:** MNL → CVG (Air Canada, Basic Economy)

---

## Air Canada Fare Classes

| Fare Class | Change Fee | Fare Difference | Notes |
|------------|------------|-----------------|-------|
| **Basic Economy** | Yes | Yes | Limited changes, strict rules |
| Economy Standard | Yes | Yes | More flexible than Basic |
| Economy Flex | No | Yes | Only pay fare difference |
| Comfort | No | Yes | Only pay fare difference |
| Latitude | No | Yes | Full flexibility |

---

## Basic Economy Ticket Rules

**Your ticket ("very basic") likely falls here:**

### Change Rules
- ✅ **Changes allowed:** Yes (but restricted)
- ❌ **Free changes:** No
- 💰 **Change fee:** $100-200 CAD (typical)
- 💰 **Fare difference:** Required if new fare > old fare
- 💳 **Payment:** Change fee + fare difference required

### What You Can Change
- ✅ Date
- ✅ Time
- ❌ Route (MNL → BKK) — **Often blocked**
- ❌ Origin city — **Blocked**
- ❌ Destination city — **Blocked**

Total:         $150 (change fee only)

## Can You Change MNL → BKK?

**Likely: NO**

### Why Route Changes Are Blocked
- Basic Economy tickets are point-to-point
- Route changes (MNL → BKK) = different fare class
- Air Canada likely blocks route changes on Basic Economy
- May require cancellation and rebooking

## Your Options

### Option 1: Call Air Canada (Best First Step)
**Ask:**
1. "Can I change MNL → CVG to BKK → CVG?"
2. "What's the change fee?"
3. "Is route change allowed on Basic Economy?"

**Possible answers:**
- ✅ Yes, route change allowed — proceed with change
- ❌ No, route change blocked — see other options
