🎭 KAGE × HIKARI — The Story
Collab duo — screaming male + melodic female · Album: KAGEKŌ
KAGE
影 — Shadow
Origin: Tokyo's underground streets. No family, no name — just a kid learning to survive in the alleys beneath the train lines. Found an old guitar at 16 and taught himself to scream because nobody was listening anyway.

The Blood Moon Night: Jumped by a crew in Shinjuku, outnumbered and bleeding against a wall. Looked up and saw the moon turn red through the smoke. Something snapped. Fought them all off with nothing but fury. Walked away with blood on his coat and Yami no Naka De in his head.

Personality: Silent outside, storm inside. Writes alone in a tiny apartment with a single red bulb. Screaming is easier than talking.
Screaming Post-Hardcore Lone Wolf
HIKARI
光 — Light
Origin: Born in a small coastal town south of Tokyo. Her family ran a ramen shop — nothing special, but full of love. When she was 12, her father got sick. Music became her escape — she'd climb to the roof and sing to the stars.

The Breaking Point: Her father passed when she was 16. She moved to Tokyo with nothing but a bag and a dream. Started singing at live houses in Shimokitazawa — her voice so raw that people stopped mid-conversation to listen.

Personality: Fierce outside, soft at the core. Fights for everyone she loves. Her motto: "Kizuna wa tsuyoi — bonds are stronger than any darkness."
Melodic J-Rock Battle Anthem
KAGEKŌ
影光 — Shadow & Light
How They Met: A dimly lit live house in Shimokitazawa. KAGE was screaming his heart out to a crowd of maybe 12 people. Half walked out. HIKARI was in the back, arms crossed. After the set, she walked up: "You're angry. I like it. But you need someone to carry the melody so the screams hit harder." He handed her a crumpled piece of paper. She read it. "Meet me tomorrow."

Their debut album KAGEKŌ (影光) — Shadow & Light, two opposites that complete each other. Coming soon.
