# VANITO — Anime Series Bible

**Genre:** Dark fantasy action · Demon Slayer × Afro Samurai
**Tone:** Lone swordsman, supernatural enemies, minimal words, maximum cool
**Format:** 3–5 min episodes, each with a complete arc + season-long thread

---

## Logline
A lone swordsman with a scar and a katana hunts the demons that wear human faces — and the one that gave him his scar is the one he's been looking for all along.

---

## The World — Nexus Protocol
A hidden order that binds warriors to elemental **Cinders** (living flames of power). Vanito was their best — the **Cinder Sovereign**, a flame-wreathed shinobi whose katana could cut through fate itself. He left after a mission went wrong: a scar on his cheek, a city he couldn't save, a promise to never draw the blade again.

The Protocol is collapsing from within. Demons wear human faces. The only way to protect the city Vanito abandoned is to become the weapon he swore off.

---

## Main Character — VANITO
- **Look (LOCKED):** Deep brown skin, muscular athletic build, short tapered/faded dark hair, light neatly-trimmed mustache + goatee (NOT a full heavy beard), oval/soft full face, broad nose, full lips. Large silver-framed **aviator sunglasses** (signature). Fitted black T-shirt. Battle **scar** across his cheek toward the corner of his mouth.
- **Weapon:** Sleek dark curved **katana** — always rendered shiny/glinting, alive with reflected light.
- **Personality:** Quiet, cold, efficient. Speaks in short sentences. Never explains himself. Carries guilt from the city he couldn't save.
- **Signature techniques (Cinder Slash forms):**
  - First Form — **Ember's Edge** (single crimson cut)
  - (more forms to be named per episode)

## Antagonist — THE WHITE DEMON
- **Look:** Figure in white, holding a flame in one palm. Human form is smooth, amused, patient. True form is a twisted shadowed thing with too many eyes.
- **Role:** Gave Vanito his scar. The season-long target. Speaks softly, always amused.
- **Motive:** Wants the Cinder Sovereign strong enough to be worth killing — or worth turning.

---

## Enemies — THE DEMONS
- Wear human faces, feed on human fear/emotion.
- True form: twisted, shadowed, too many glowing eyes.
- Each episode = one demon, one technique, one step closer to the White Demon.

## Recurring Elements
- **Rain** — the show's texture. Heavy, layered, constant.
- **Neon city** — rain-slicked streets, pink/blue neon, bokeh lights.
- **The scar** — glows faintly when the White Demon is near.
- **Embers** — drift after every kill; the flame never fully dies.

## Sound Design (every episode)
- **SFX:** katana whoosh (draw + slash), impact boom, demon dissolve (crackling embers), rain ambience, wet footsteps, title sting (katana-slice).
- **Screen flashes:** white/red flash frames on impact strikes (classic anime technique, layered in ffmpeg).
- **Music:** Afro Samurai-style minimalism — sparse cello, slow-building drums, silence on the kill.
