# VANITO — Episode 1: "The Scar"

**Runtime:** ~4:30 | **Style:** Demon Slayer × Afro Samurai | **Tone:** Lone swordsman, supernatural enemies, minimal words, maximum cool

---

## COLD OPEN — "The Walk" (0:00–0:20)

**[SCENE 1 — EXT. NEON ALLEY, NIGHT]**

*Rain. Heavy. The only sound is water hitting concrete and a distant train.*

**SHOT 1 (WIDE, LOW ANGLE):** A single figure walks through a neon-lit alley. VANITO — black tee, aviators, katana across his shoulder. Rain beads off his sunglasses. He doesn't look at the camera.

**SHOT 2 (CLOSE, PROFILE):** The katana glints as it catches a neon sign — pink, then blue. The blade is *alive* with reflected light.

**SHOT 3 (EXTREME CLOSE, EYES):** Behind the aviators, his eyes are cold. Focused. He's not walking home. He's hunting.

> **SFX:** Rain (heavy, layered), footsteps on wet concrete, distant train horn.
> **MUSIC:** Low, sparse — a single cello note, held. Afro Samurai-style minimalism.

**TITLE CARD:** *VANITO* — slams in with a katana-slice sound. Red ink bleeds down the screen.

---

## ACT I — "The Hunt" (0:20–1:20)

**[SCENE 2 — EXT. RAIN-SLICKED STREET]**

**SHOT 4 (MEDIUM):** Vanito stops. A man in a suit stands under a streetlight, umbrella up, smiling too wide. THE DEMON (human form).

> **DEMON:** "You're a long way from home, swordsman."

*Vanito says nothing. He keeps walking.*

> **DEMON:** *(laughing)* "What's the matter? Cat got your tongue? Or did a demon get your *city*?"

*Vanito stops. The scar on his cheek catches the light.*

**SHOT 5 (CLOSE, VANITO):** His hand tightens on the katana hilt. One beat of silence.

> **VANITO:** *(quiet)* "You talk too much."

**SHOT 6 (WIDE):** The demon's smile splits — too wide, too far. His body twists, skin peeling back like shadow. **TRUE FORM:** a twisted, shadowed thing with too many eyes, all of them glowing.

> **DEMON:** "Then let's not talk."

> **SFX:** Umbrella drops, demon's body cracking/reshaping, rain intensifies.
> **MUSIC:** Drums enter — slow, building. A heartbeat.

---

## ACT II — "The Technique" (1:20–3:00)

**[SCENE 3 — EXT. STREET, THE CLASH]**

**SHOT 7 (LOW ANGLE, WIDE):** Vanito draws the katana. One motion. The blade catches the neon — **shiny, glinting, alive.** Rain splits around the edge.

**SHOT 8 (ACTION, FAST CUTS):** The demon lunges. Vanito moves like water — a blur, a step, a breath. The fight is a *dance*, not a brawl.

**SHOT 9 (CLOSE, THE BLADE):** Crimson energy coils along the edge.

> **VANITO:** *(whisper)* "Cinder Slash — First Form. **Ember's Edge.**"

**SHOT 10 (IMPACT, SLOW-MO):** A single cut. A crimson arc carves through the rain. The demon freezes — then splits, dissolving into ash and embers that scatter on the wind.

> **SFX:** Katana whoosh (sharp, metallic), impact boom, demon dissolving into crackling embers.
> **MUSIC:** Drums peak, then cut to silence on the kill.

**[FLASHBACK — SCENE 4 — EXT. BURNING CITY, NIGHT]**

*The screen warps. Fire. Screaming. A younger Vanito, katana broken, standing before a burning city.*

**SHOT 11 (WIDE):** A figure in WHITE stands in the flames — THE WHITE DEMON. It turns. Its eyes lock on Vanito.

**SHOT 12 (CLOSE, VANITO):** A claw rakes across his cheek. Blood. The scar is born.

> **WHITE DEMON:** *(soft, amused)* "You're not strong enough yet, Sovereign. Come find me when you are."

*The flames swallow everything.*

**[BACK TO PRESENT — SCENE 3 CONTINUED]**

**SHOT 13 (CLOSE, VANITO):** Vanito stands over the ash, breathing hard. The scar on his cheek *burns*. He doesn't sheathe the katana.

> **SFX:** Embers crackling, rain returning, Vanito's ragged breath.
> **MUSIC:** A single low cello note returns. Ominous.

---

## ACT III — "The Name" (3:00–4:30)

**[SCENE 5 — EXT. STREET, THE REVEAL]**

**SHOT 14 (WIDE):** A voice from the shadows.

> **VOICE:** *(smooth, familiar)* "You've gotten slower, Sovereign."

*Vanito turns. A figure steps out of the dark — THE WHITE DEMON, human form now, holding a flame in one palm. The same demon from the flashback.*

**SHOT 15 (CLOSE, VANITO):** His jaw tightens. The katana glints.

> **VANITO:** *(low)* "You."

> **WHITE DEMON:** "I've been waiting for you to get strong enough to find me. And now — you have."

*It smiles. The flame in its palm flickers.*

> **WHITE DEMON:** "Every demon you kill, every technique you learn — it's all been leading here. To me. To the one who gave you that scar."

**SHOT 16 (EXTREME CLOSE, THE SCAR):** The scar on Vanito's cheek glows faintly — reacting to the demon's presence.

> **VANITO:** *(quiet, steel)* "Then you won't have to wait much longer."

**SHOT 17 (WIDE):** The White Demon laughs — and dissolves into embers, scattering on the wind. Vanito stands alone in the rain, katana still drawn, the scar burning.

**SHOT 18 (FINAL, SLOW PUSH-IN):** Vanito sheathes the katana. Turns. Walks into the rain. The embers of the dead demon still drift around him.

> **VANITO:** *(voiceover, cold)* "The hunt is on."

**CUT TO BLACK.**

> **SFX:** Embers fading, rain, footsteps receding.
> **MUSIC:** The cello resolves into a single, final note. Hold. Fade.

**END CARD:** *VANITO — Episode 2: "The White Demon" — Next time.*
