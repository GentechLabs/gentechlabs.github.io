How this wallet was funded — our own bridge, from zero

The wallet held no USDC at all before this, and Arc charges fees in USDC — the classic bootstrap problem: you need the asset to receive the asset, if you submit the destination mint yourself. We used CCTP's Forwarding Service instead: the burn on Avalanche carried a forward request, so Circle broadcast the Arc mint from its own keys. The destination needed no gas, no signing. The first thing that balance did was pay for its own contract deployment.
