# Beginner's Guide to the Bull Market — Start With Bitcoin

> GenTech EDU · Module 1 · Created 2026-09-01
> For family and friends who are new to crypto. Plain language, no jargon walls.
> Core idea: **in a bull market, every dip is an opportunity — not a knife.**
> But you only know that if you can read the market. That's what this guide teaches.

---

## Why this guide exists

Most beginners lose money for one reason: they buy the top because everyone is
excited, then they sell the bottom because everyone is scared. They are always
reacting to feelings.

The fix is not a secret coin pick. It's learning to read **market sentiment** —
the mood of the whole market — and letting that, not your emotions, decide when
you move. This guide teaches you the exact tools we use on the GenTech treasury,
scaled down for a beginner.

## Lesson 1 — Zoom Out First (Daily Lies, Monthly Tells)

If you stare at the daily chart, every day feels like a crisis. Zoom out.

| Window | What it tells you | Beginner rule |
|--------|------------------|---------------|
| **1d** | Today's noise | Never make decisions on this alone |
| **7d** | This week's direction | Context for the daily move |
| **1m** | The actual trend | This is the truth-teller |

**Worked example (Sep 1, 2026):** BTC showed **-0.8% today** — scary on the
daily. Zoom out: **+22.8% over 30 days**. The daily was noise; the monthly
showed a market reversing upward. That's why we zoom out before we feel
anything about a red day.

**The 200-day moving average (200d MA):** the average price of the last 200
days. It's the market's long memory.
- Price **above** the 200d MA → the long trend is up (bull territory)
- Price **below** it → the long trend is down or healing

BTC at $78,008 vs a 200d MA of ~$69,472 = **+12% above** → long trend is up.
That's the foundation everything else builds on.

## Lesson 2 — Reading Sentiment: The Fear & Greed Index

The [Fear & Greed Index](https://alternative.me/crypto/fear-and-greed-index/)
boils the market's mood into one number from 0 to 100:

| Value | Mood | What it means |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 0–24 | 🔴 Extreme Fear | People are panicking. Historically where fortunes are made |
| 25–44 | 🟠 Fear | Nervous selling, good staggered entries |
| 45–55 | 🟡 Neutral | No edge either way |
| 56–75 | 🟢 Greed | People are buying happily. Dips get bought fast, but don't chase |
| 76–100 | 🔴 Extreme Greed | Party is loud. This is where you take profits, not add |

**The contrarian rule:** buy when others are fearful, be careful when others
are greedy. Warren Buffett's rule, and it works.

**But don't be a robot.** Greed (60–80) during an early bull trend is *normal* —
the trend can keep running for months while sentiment is warm. Greed only
becomes dangerous when it hits extremes AND price is far above trend. Read the
mood **with** the trend, never alone.

**Worked example:** F&G today = **69 (Greed)**, up from 62 yesterday. Warm,
not euphoric. Combined with the uptrend from Lesson 1, the market says:
*"healthy bull, but don't chase — let dips come to you."*

## Lesson 3 — Buy Zones: Our Value Ladder

We never buy "all at once." We set **zones** — price levels where a coin becomes
a better deal. GenTech's buy list uses four labels:

| Zone | Meaning | What we do |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 🔥 **Deep Value** | Price is unusually cheap vs its range | Strongest buy zone |
| 🟢 **Accumulate** | Fair-to-good value | Buy in small steps (DCA) |
| 🔵 **Watch** | Neutral. Nothing special | Do nothing, watch |
| ⚪ **Extended** | Price is rich vs recent range | Don't buy. Maybe take profit |

**Worked example:** BTC is at $78,008 = **Extended** zone (our watch line is
$76K, extended above $84K is the breakout trigger). So the buy list says:
extended — do NOT chase. Our tiered bids sit below:
- **$71–72K** — first add zone
- **$66–67K** — second add
- **$60–61K** — the "gift zone" (still above the long-term trend line)

A beginner with $100 does NOT need to decide "is it going up or down?" They set
three small orders at those zones and let the market come to them.

## Lesson 4 — The DeFi Rainbow (Value at a Glance)

The rainbow is a color mood-ring for each coin, built from its 7d and 30d
momentum:

- 🟣 **Panic Farm** — generational cheap. Be greedy when others fear.
- 🔵 **Bleeding Edge** — cheap. Start micro-buys.
- 🟢 **Accumulation** — fair value. Steady DCA.
- 🟡 **Harvest Mode** — healthy. Hold and compound.
- 🟠 **Peak Yield** — getting rich. Trim into strength.
- 🔴 **Euphoria** — take profits. Price is stretched.

Beginner mapping: 🟣/🔵 = buy zones, 🟢/🟡 = hold, 🟠/🔴 = sell zones.

## Lesson 5 — How to Buy a Dip (Without Catching a Knife)

A "knife" is a falling price you buy too early and it keeps falling. The
protection is boring and it works:

1. **Never deploy everything at once.** Split your buy into 3 parts (a ladder).
2. **Buy in tiers, not at one price.** Example: 30% at the first dip, 30% at
   the next zone, 40% at the deep zone.
3. **Only buy dips in an uptrend** (price above 200d MA). Dips in a downtrend
   are not opportunities — they are the trend.
4. **Check sentiment before each buy.** Extreme Fear + uptrend = best combo
   in crypto. Extreme Greed + extended price = wait.
5. **Time is on your side.** Missing the exact bottom is fine. Ladders catch
   the average, not the bottom.

**The one-sentence version:** *in a confirmed uptrend, buy the fear in tiers,
not the excitement in one shot.*

## Lesson 6 — Where a Beginner Actually Starts

1. **Start with Bitcoin.** It's the tide every other coin swims in. If BTC is
   healthy, altcoins breathe. If BTC breaks down, everything follows.
2. **Spot only. No leverage.** Leverage is how beginners get liquidated on a
   dip that would have recovered. Borrowed money turns an opportunity into a
   liquidation.
3. **Small size.** An amount you could lose without changing your life. The
   first year is for learning, not earning.
4. **Set the ladder before you look at the price again.** Decide your tiers
   when you're calm, not when the chart is bleeding.
5. **Zoom out weekly.** Check the 1d if you're curious, decide on the 1m.

## The Steward's Beginner Card

```
1. Is price above the 200d MA?           → no: wait, or tiny DCA only
2. What does Fear & Greed say?           → extreme fear = gift, extreme greed = trim
3. Where is price vs the buy zones?      → extended = don't chase
4. Ladder set? (3 tiers, never all-in)   → no ladder, no buy
5. Spot only, no leverage                → always, until experienced
```

Print this. Tape it somewhere. This card is the whole course.
