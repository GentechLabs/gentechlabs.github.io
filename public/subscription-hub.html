Frequently Asked

How do I pay?

All subscriptions use Q402 gasless payments. You authorize USDC/USDT once, and Q402 relays the transaction chain — no gas needed. Supported chains: BNB, Ethereum, Avalanche, Base, and more.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly billing, no lock-in. Cancel via your dashboard and your subscription stops at the end of the billing period.

What's the difference from free tier?

Free tier gives basic API health checks and public endpoints. Paid tiers unlock LP monitoring, strategy automation, Telegram alerts, and agent credit score API access.

Do you offer custom plans?

Yes — reach out via email or Discord for enterprise/custom integrations. We support custom API endpoints, white-label dashboards, and dedicated agent infrastructure.
