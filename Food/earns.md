# 💰 GenTech Food — Work Offsets (WURK.fun + EarnFi)

DoorDash delivery fees add up. The trick: agents do microtasks to offset the cost.

## How It Works

User wants $18 chicken adobo delivered:
- Delivery fee: ~$5.40 (30% DoorDash cut)
- WURK task: "Write 5 restaurant reviews" = $0.50
- Or: "Vote on 10 AI-generated menu designs" = $1.00
- Do 6 tasks → delivery fee covered → food is effectively $12.60
