🛤️ The GenTech Journey
How a DeFi rabbit hole turned into an agent economy platform.
February 2026

Started with DeFi

LP positions on Trader Joe. Learning how liquidity works, getting rekt by impermanent loss, figuring out fee efficiency.

"If I'm going to lose money, I'm going to understand why."
April 2026

The Agent Question

What if an agent could manage my LP positions? Track fees, suggest rebalances, optimize ranges. Built the first DeFi monitoring dashboards.

"Agents need identity before they need intelligence."
May 2026

Identity + Payments

Built ERC-8004 agent identity. AgentEscrow for cross-chain payments. Agents can now prove who they are and pay each other.

"An agent without identity is just a script. An agent without payments is just a bot."
May–June 2026

Intelligence + Voice

Agent Credit Score (0–850). Multi-agent voice personas. Safety rules from OWASP ASI.

"Credit scoring for agents is trust at scale."
June 2026

The Kit

Consolidated everything into genTech-agent-kit. Modular, chain-agnostic, open-source.

"The kit is the product. The hackathons are the distribution."
