## ADDENDUM — CLARITY Act Not on Monday Senate Schedule (Aug 3)

**Source:** [Watcher.Guru tweet](https://x.com/i/status/2084098458495942780)

The Senate's Monday schedule does **not** include the Crypto (Digital Asset Market) Clarity Act — H.R. 3633. This matters because the **hard deadline is Aug 7** (final Senate workday before August recess). Lummis still says it gets a floor vote before recess, but it's now a knife-edge scramble.

**Why this is a macro/crypto signal, not just DC news:**
- The CLARITY Act is the **market-structure companion** to the GENIUS Act (stablecoin law passed last year). It gives CFTC primary jurisdiction over "digital commodities" (BTC, ETH, SOL, AVAX, LINK), sets registration rules for exchanges/dealers, and clarifies when crypto is a security vs commodity.
- Passing it before recess = **regulatory clarity** for the whole spot + derivatives market → bullish tailwind (institutions can treat these as commodities with clear rules).
- Slipping past Aug 7 = **killed until after recess** (mid-Sept). Not a crash catalyst, but removes a near-term bullish overhang and keeps uncertainty (and the SEC/CFTC jurisdictional ambiguity) alive.

**GTA implication:** Fear 27 + flat BTC + a big regulatory catalyst slipping off the calendar = **stay flat / capital preservation**. No reason to add risk while both geopolitical (Hormuz) and legislative (CLARITY) headlines are unresolved. A floor vote landing Tue–Thu this week would be a real risk-on trigger to watch.
