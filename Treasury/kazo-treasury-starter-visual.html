⚠️ THE RULE WE WON'T BREAK AGAIN

Every wallet you create must store its own private key — or point only to a wallet you control. A funded wallet with no key is a failure, not an oversight. We stranding $33.63 on a keyless wallet once. The provisioner now flags it, structurally, on every run. The guardrail is in the code, not in a reminder.
