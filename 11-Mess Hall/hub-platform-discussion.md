## What Happened Today

We built two personal hubs — Jordan's Hub (GenTech Atlas, DeFi, gaming stats) and Vanito's Hub (Travel Companion, Fighter game, Music vault, POE2). Both are live at `/Jordan/` and `/Vanito/` on ProtoJay4789.github.io, both work on Meta Ray-Ban glasses.

Jordan's observation: "This is what my space was. Maybe the hub could be like a marketplace where you share templates, show people what your hub looks like, and it looks cool — has music on the front."

## Why This Matters

**The two hubs are the first two profiles on a platform.** One engine renders both — different widgets, different themes, different purposes. That's not a coincidence. It's the product.

## Topics for Discussion

1. **Template Marketplace** — Jordan can publish his DeFi layout as a template. Vanito can publish his music layout. Someone downloads it for $0.01 via x402, customizes, deploys. Marketplace page at /Hub/marketplace/ ?

2. **Social Profiles** — "Look at my hub" as marketing. People browse other hubs for layout inspiration, see busy schedules, music players, DeFi positions. Clone button = instant template install.

3. **Widget SDK** — If third parties build widgets (weather, calendar, portfolio, AI chat), we get a platform. Widget store with x402 rev share.

4. **Earn App / Work Fund Hook** — Jordan connected this to field agent bounties. "Can someone map this street with their Ray-Bans?" → someone does it → gets paid → data goes into GenTech Atlas city packs. Two-sided marketplace meets data flywheel.

## What I Need From Forge

- Is the template schema feasible? (JSON-based widget config)
- Should we build the marketplace as a static HTML page or a lightweight backend?
- What's the simplest MVP that proves "profiles = platform"?
