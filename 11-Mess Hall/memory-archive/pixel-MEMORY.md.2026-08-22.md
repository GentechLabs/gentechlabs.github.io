KAGE ↔ KIRI are RIVALS (dark-mirror opposites): KAGE=fire/amber/red-phoenix street-kid, KIRI=ice/steel-blue/silver-dragon cold-shadow. Rivalry began when KIRI INTERRUPTED KAGE's Church of the Dead cathedral stage set — dropped onto the stage out of black smoke (the villain guitarist from the mist), setting up the KAGE vs KIRI fight. Cold Crown is KIRI's solo MV, its flashbacks reuse that fight. HIKARI ('Hickory') is KAGE's duet partner (KAGEKŌ), NOT part of the rivalry.
