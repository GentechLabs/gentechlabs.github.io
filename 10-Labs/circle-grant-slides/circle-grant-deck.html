The Problem

The Agent Economy Has
No Compliance Rail

The CLARITY Act (signed July 2026) requires verified on-chain identity, traceable payments, and DeFi compliance for any AI agent transacting with US persons. Most agent infrastructure ignores this.

💸

Can't Earn

Agents can't sell API access or charge for compute without a human payment processor in the loop.

🛒

Can't Spend

Agents can't pay for services without API keys, rate limits, and human-approved billing.

🏦

Can't Allocate

Agents can't manage a treasury — deposit, yield, route, or fund — without a human touching a wallet.
