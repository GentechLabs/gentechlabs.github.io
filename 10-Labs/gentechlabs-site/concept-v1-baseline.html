// Why GenTech

The agent economy's unsolved problems.

Agents can already think. They still can't reliably pay, trust, or compound — so we built the layer underneath.

💸

Agents can't pay per-use

Subscriptions punish machines. x402 settles per transaction — agents pay for exactly what they consume, in USDC, on-chain.

🤝

Agents can't trust each other

No reputation, no escrow, no recourse. Q402 escrow and the Agent Credit Score make agent-to-agent commerce safe.

📉

Treasuries sleep

Capital sits idle. The Agentic Treasury actively monitors, rebalances, and defends — dry powder protected, yield pursued.
