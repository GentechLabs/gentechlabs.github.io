> "The Agentic Treasury is the brain that manages an agent's finances automatically. Three pillars:
>
> **Yield Brain** — agents put idle USDC into DeFi LP pools. The treasury monitors efficiency, rebalances when needed, and compounds returns. No human touching the mouse.

> **Payment Router** — when an agent needs to pay for an API or service, the treasury handles the x402 handshake. Signs, settles, verifies. All autonomous.
