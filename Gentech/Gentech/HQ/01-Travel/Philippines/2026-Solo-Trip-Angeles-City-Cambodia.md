## Flight Research — Angeles City → Cambodia

### Route Options

| Route | Distance | Flight Time | Airline | Est Cost |
|-------|----------|-------------|---------|----------|
| **CRK → PNH** (Clark → Phnom Penh) | 1,800 km | 3h 15min | Cebu Pacific (via MNL) | $120-180 |
| **CRK → REP** (Clark → Siem Reap) | 1,900 km | 3h 30min | Cebu Pacific (via MNL) | $140-200 |
| **MNL → PNH** (Manila → Phnom Penh) | 1,800 km | 3h | Cebu Pacific, AirAsia | $80-150 |
| **MNL → REP** (Manila → Siem Reap) | 1,900 km | 3h 15min | Cebu Pacific, AirAsia | $100-170 |

### Flight Pattern

**Direct flights:**
- MNL ↔ PNH: Daily (Cebu Pacific, AirAsia)
- MNL ↔ REP: Daily (Cebu Pacific, AirAsia)

**From Clark (CRK):**
- No direct flights to Cambodia
- Must route via Manila (MNL)
- CRK → MNL (45min) + MNL → Cambodia (3h) = ~4h total

### Cost Estimates

| Route | Budget | Mid-range | Notes |
|-------|--------|-----------|-------|
| **CRK → PNH** | $120 | $150 | Via MNL, 4h total |
| **CRK → REP** | $140 | $170 | Via MNL, 4h 15min total |
| **Return** | $120-140 | $150-170 | Same route back |

## Logistics

### Angeles City → Cambodia

- Cebu Pacific: 45min, $20-40

**Step 3:** MNL layover: 1-3h
