## What it is
Rain (enterprise stablecoin payments platform, Visa + Mastercard principal member) launched the **Agentic Payments Alliance (APA)** — a working coalition of **26 founding members** across payments, stablecoins, and AI, run collectively (not owned by one company).

**Founding members:** Avalanche, Basis Theory, Chainalysis, Circle, Coinflow, Crossmint, delta Network, Episode Six, Evertec, Fireblocks, Fiserv, Kala, Lithic, Mastercard, Monad, PayOS, Rain, Remitly, Rialo by Subzero Labs, Sardine, Shift4, Solana, Turnkey, Uniswap Labs, Visa, Yuno.

## The stated problems (map to GenTech)
By 2030 McKinsey projects $3–5T agentic commerce. The APA exists to shape, before decisions get made in isolation:
1. **How agents get authorized** → our ERC-8004 identity / a2a governance
2. **How fraud gets caught** → our Mastercard challenge pre-execution governance guard
3. **How loyalty/rewards travel with an agent**

## GenTech relevance
- Proof-of-concept for our **orchestrator / a2a** thesis: the industry explicitly concedes "no single company should decide how agents transact."
- Same axis as our Mastercard Innovation Challenge build (pre-execution fraud governance).
- **Agentic Startup Program** — Rain accelerator, first cohort of 5 presenting at demo day open to members. A realistic on-ramp for a smaller operator like us (vs full enterprise membership).

## Honest caveat
- Membership is **organizational** (payments/fintech/policy). Joining as a 2-person op is a stretch now.
- **Watch:** whether emerging agent-identity standards align with or oppose ERC-8004 (which we're building on). Track.
