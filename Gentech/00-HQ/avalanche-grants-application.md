### 3. Retro9000 — Retroactive Funding

**Program:** Avalanche Foundation Retro9000 ($40M total pool)
**Application URL:** https://retro9000.avax.network/

**Two active rounds:**
- **C-Chain Round 5** — Submission deadline **July 17, 2026 at 6:00 PM UTC** — rewards onchain C-Chain activity (AVAX burned from tx fees). Reward pool: up to 10,000 AVAX (or total AVAX burned during round). Top 20 projects eligible.
- **L1s & Infrastructure Tooling** — **Continuous/rolling round** — deploy L1s or build developer tooling. Up to $40M worth of AVAX.

**Product fit:** x402 Gateway Avalanche Support (infrastructure)

**Key Program Details:**
- Yes, Retro9000 **does fund infrastructure projects** (not just DApps)
- Since Nov 2024: 195 projects joined, $1M+ awarded to 19 projects in first cohort
- Community voting matters — projects ranked on leaderboard
- First snapshot was April 2025; quarterly snapshots ongoing
- Referral rewards available ($100 → $500–$10,000 in AVAX for referring projects)

**User verification tiers (C-Chain rounds):**
- Unregistered → least weight
- Connected (wallet + 30-day-old tx) → more weight
- Verified (wallet + 30-day-old tx + X account linked) → most weight
