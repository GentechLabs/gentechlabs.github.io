## What is Retro9000?

Retro9000 is a $40M retroactive grant initiative from the Avalanche Foundation, rewarding builders and referrers driving real impact onchain. Launched November 2024.

**Key facts:**
- 195 projects joined, $1M+ awarded to 19 projects in first cohort
- 1.3M unique voters casting nearly 10B votes
- Quarterly snapshots (next: October 14, 2025 at 12:00 PM UTC for original L1 round)
- **Yes, Retro9000 funds infrastructure projects** (not just DApps)

## Active Rounds

### C-Chain Round 5

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Submission deadline | **July 17, 2026 at 6:00 PM UTC** |
| Reward pool | Up to 10,000 AVAX (or total AVAX burned during round) |
| Eligibility | Top 20 projects on leaderboard |
| Scoring | Based on AVAX burned from transaction fees |
| User tiers | Unregistered → Connected → Verified (tiers affect score weight) |
| New project multiplier | 5x for new projects, 5x for Build Games projects |
| How it works | Build on C-Chain → drive tx volume → community votes → ranked on leaderboard |

**User verification tiers:**
- **Unregistered** — users who interact onchain but haven't connected to Retro9000 platform → least weight
- **Connected** — wallet connected + at least one tx on Avalanche that's ≥30 days old → more weight
- **Verified** — wallet connected + 30-day-old tx + X account linked (X account ≥30 days old) → most weight

**Recommendation for C-Chain Round 5:** ❌ **Skip** — rewards tx volume/AVAX burn, which is our weak spot. We don't have enough onchain activity yet.
