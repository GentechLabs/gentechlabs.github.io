## How to use this template

### Ranking axes
1. **Difficulty** — barrier to entry (skills, scarcity of entry roles, market volatility)
2. **Income potential** — realistic range + ceiling + variance
3. **Fit-to-candidate** — does the candidate's existing stack lower the barrier?
