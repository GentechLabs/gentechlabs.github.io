## Storybeat (10s)
- **0-3s:** Embermaw lunges and swipes — claw catches Lightning's arm, deep gash opens, BLOOD POURS SLOWLY down his coat sleeve onto the cobblestones. He grimaces but holds.
- **3-6s:** Lightning grits his teeth, raises the BLOOD WICK (curved grey-steel blade, two lit blood-red candles on the spine, blood dripping off the edge), charges through the fog, dodging claws, coat billowing.
- **6-10s:** Lightning leaps and drives the Blood Wick deep into Embermaw's burning chest wound. Golden-red light erupts, the beast roars its last, staggers, and collapses — SLAIN in a spreading pool of blood, embers rising from its fur. Lightning lands, blood still dripping from his wound, standing over the fallen boss. No victory pose.
