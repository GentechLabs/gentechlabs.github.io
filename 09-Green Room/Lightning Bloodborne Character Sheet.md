# LIGHTNING — Bloodborne Hunter Character Reference

## Name
Lightning

## Species
Human — Bloodborne-style hunter in the Gothic horror universe

## Appearance
- **Build**: Tall (6'1"–6'4"), heavy-set / burly / endomorph, broad shoulders, thick neck, rounded torso, thick forearms
- **Skin**: Fair to light-olive, smooth
- **Eyes**: Medium dark-brown, hooded eyelids, straight thick dark brows
- **Hair**: Short dark-brown/off-black textured crop, forward-brushed fringe cut straight across the brow, sides trimmed short
- **Facial hair**: DISCONNECTED GOATEE — clean-shaven cheeks, thin dark mustache with gap at the philtrum, medium-length neatly squared dark tuft below the lower lip on the chin
- **Expression**: Neutral, stoic

## Outfit (Bloodborne theme)
- Long dark leather Victorian hunter coat, high collar, double-breasted brass buttons, shoulder straps/epaulets, belted waist with square buckle, sleeve straps
- Coat weathered, hem tattered/shredded and soaked in blood, blood spatters on sleeves, hands, chest
- Charcoal collared dress shirt (partially unbuttoned at throat)
- Black trousers tucked into knee-high dark leather hunter boots with ankle straps/buckles
- Dark cloth sash
- NO modern vest, NO cargo shorts — full Gothic Victorian hunter

## Weapon — The Blood Wick (trick weapon)
- Heavy curved cleaver-style blade, thick, coated in fresh blood dripping off the edge
- Two lit blood-red wax ritual candles mounted on the spine/guard, active flames, molten red wax dripping onto crossguard and blade
- Leather-wrapped grip, ornate metallic pommel
- Brutal one-handed trick weapon

## Signature Poses

2. Left profile — silhouette of coat, fringe, goatee
3. Back — coat detail, hem, boots
4. Weapon inset — Blood Wick close-up

## Personality (inferred)
- Stoic, grim, relentless — a hunter of the night
