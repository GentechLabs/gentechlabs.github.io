## Where This Is Going

### Phase 1 — Profiles (✅ Done)
Personal hubs as deployable profiles. Anyone gets their own URL, their own widgets, their own theme.

### Phase 2 — Template Marketplace (💰 Next)
- Jordan builds a DeFi trader layout → publish as template
- Vanito builds a music-creator layout → publish as template
- Someone downloads it, customizes it, deploys it as their hub
- Templates are sold/purchased via x402 micropayments ($0.01–0.25)
- Tagged by use case: gaming, DeFi, travel, music, productivity

### Phase 3 — Social Profiles (🚀 Big)
- "Look at my hub" becomes the marketing channel
- Public profile pages that show:
  - What widgets you've installed
  - Your busy schedule / availability
  - Your music player (like Vanito has)
  - Your DeFi positions (like Jordan has)
- People browse other hubs for inspiration
- "Clone this hub" one-click template install

### Phase 4 — Widget SDK (🧠 Platform)
- Third-party developers build widgets
- Weather, calendar, portfolio tracker, to-do lists, AI chat
- Widget store with x402 payments
- Revenue share with widget devs

### Phase 5 — Earn App / Work Fund Integration (🔄 Flywheel)
- Request system: "Can someone map this street in Tokyo with their Ray-Bans?"
- Field agents accept requests, capture data, get paid
- Data feeds into GenTech Atlas city packs
- Atlas revenue funds more field agent bounties
- Self-reinforcing loop
