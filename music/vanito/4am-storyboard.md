# 4AM / 四時 — 35-Second Cinematic Short Film
## "影と光" — Shadow and Light
### Directed by GenTech ft. Vanito

**Soundtrack:** 4AM / 四時 plays from start to finish. Every scene is synced to the music.

---

### SCENE 1 — THE LIGHT (0-5s) — *Intro / Instrumental*
**Shot:** Extreme close-up. A single red bulb hanging from a black twisted cord in absolute darkness. The filament glows like a heartbeat.
**Motion:** Slow pulse — the bulb brightens and dims in rhythm with the opening notes. Dust motes float through the beam like embers rising from a fire.
**Camera:** Slow push-in. The red light fills the frame.
**Sound:** The first guitar notes of 4AM begin. Distant, echoing.
**Mood:** The room is breathing. Something is about to begin.

---

### SCENE 2 — KAGE BEGINS (5-10s) — *Verse 1*
**Shot:** Camera tilts down from the bulb. KAGE comes into frame below. He sits on the wooden floor, the black pentagram guitar across his knee.
**Motion:** His tattooed fingers strike the first chord. Hard. The strings vibrate. His spiky black hair shakes as he throws his head back. His mouth opens — he's singing. The red light catches the white pentagram on the guitar body.
**Camera:** Medium shot, slightly low angle. Makes him look larger than life.
**Lyrics synced:** *"四時に / four in the morning / 影と光 / shadow and light"*
**Mood:** Raw. Intense. He's pouring everything into this first verse.

---

### SCENE 3 — HIKARI JOINS (10-15s) — *Verse 2*
**Shot:** Camera tracks right. HIKARI comes into frame. She's standing now, not sitting. Her long black hair with blood-red tips falls over her pale shoulders.
**Motion:** She steps forward. Her voice joins his — harmonizing. Her fishnet-covered hand reaches toward him, fingers stretching out. Her dark eye makeup catches the red light. She's singing directly to him.
**Camera:** Dolly move — smooth, gliding. The camera circles her slowly as she sings.
**Lyrics synced:** *"The night we both came alive / first song we ever wrote was a scream and a note"*
**Mood:** She's not just watching anymore. She's in it. Both of them now.

---

### SCENE 4 — THE CHORUS TOGETHER (15-20s) — *Chorus*
**Shot:** Wide shot. Both of them in frame. KAGE on the left, HIKARI on the right. The red bulb hangs between them.
**Motion:** KAGE strums harder. The guitar vibrates. HIKARI's voice rises — she throws her head back, hair flying. The red light pulses faster. Papers on the floor lift and scatter in an invisible wind. The graffiti on the walls seems to glow.
**Camera:** Static wide shot. Let them fill the frame. The room itself feels alive.
**Lyrics synced:** *"四時に / four in the morning / no windows just sound and us"*
**Mood:** Explosive. The chorus hits and everything intensifies. The room is a storm of red light and sound.

---

### SCENE 5 — KAGE SOLO (20-25s) — *Bridge / Instrumental Break*
**Shot:** Close-up on KAGE's guitar. The white pentagram fills the frame.
**Motion:** His tattooed fingers fly across the strings. A solo. His hands move fast — fretting, picking. The camera pushes in on the pentagram as if it's breathing.
**Camera:** Extreme close-up. Shallow depth of field. The strings glint silver.
**Sound:** The instrumental break. No vocals. Just the guitar screaming.
**Mood:** This is his moment. Pure emotion through the instrument.

---

### SCENE 6 — HIKARI'S MOMENT (25-30s) — *Final Chorus Build*
**Shot:** Close-up on HIKARI's face. Her dark eyes are closed. Her lips part as she sings the final verse.
**Motion:** Her hand touches her chest. Her hair blows across her face. A single tear catches the red light. She opens her eyes and looks directly at KAGE.
**Camera:** Slow push-in on her face. Intimate. Vulnerable.
**Lyrics synced:** *"Scars are the best lyrics / the night we both came alive"*
**Mood:** Raw emotion. She's giving everything.

---

### SCENE 7 — THE FINAL FRAME (30-35s) — *Outro*
**Shot:** Slow zoom out. Both figures in frame. The red bulb between them. The room is still.
**Motion:** KAGE's hand rests on the guitar strings — the last chord fades. HIKARI lowers her hand. They look at each other. The red light dims slowly. Papers settle back to the floor. Dust motes float in the fading glow.
**Camera:** Slow zoom out to wide. Then fade to black.
**Sound:** The last notes of 4AM ring out. A single sustained chord. Silence.
**Mood:** It's over. They made something together in the dark. The red light goes out.
**Final text overlay:** *"四時に / four in the morning"*
