[Verse 1][Soft → Build]

声が響いてた voice echoed bold

君は叫んでた you were screaming out loud
誰もいないのに to nobody at all

Something in your voice moved me deep inside

Because I know what it's like to scream and no one cares

I picked up your words, 拾ったんだ I was sworn
Nobody heard you? 聞こえてたよ I was listening right there
I heard your voice, 今も響いてる it's still ringing in the air

駅のホームで on the platform, couldn't put it down
Your kanji messy, 崩れた文字 broken letters on the page

I read your dark and found out how to feel

[Chorus][Scream][Powerful]

I heard your voice — もう離さない never leaving tonight
