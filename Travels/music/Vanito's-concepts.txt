Vanito's Concepts Songs

HIKARI Song Concepts

1 - Sakura no Chikai (桜の誓い) — Cherry Blossom Promise
About the promise she made to her father. Slow build, emotional J-Rock. Starts quiet like a lullaby, explodes into a battle cry.

2 - Taiyō no Senshi (太陽の戦士) — Sun Warrior
Full battle anthem. About being the one who stands in front and takes the hit. Fast tempo, driving drums, melodic power.

3 - Kono Sora no Shita (この空の下) — Under This Sky
A duet-ready solo about finding someone in a world of 8 billion people. Mid-tempo, warm, emotional. About the moment she walked up to KAGE at the bar.
