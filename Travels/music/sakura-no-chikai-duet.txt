Sakura no Chikai (桜の誓い) — Cherry Blossom Promise
KAGE × HIKARI Duet

[INTRO — female, soft gentle hum, warm and distant like a lullaby]
FEMALE: Mm-mm, mm-mm...

[VERSE 1 — female, quiet and fragile, voice trembling like holding back tears while remembering something precious]
FEMALE:
I remember spring, the cherry trees in bloom
You held my hand and chased away the gloom
The petals fell like snow around your chair
I didn't know that year would be your last one there

[PRE-CHORUS — female, soft but rising, voice starting to crack, fighting to stay composed]
FEMALE:
You looked at me with tired eyes, but smiled just the same
"Don't you cry for me," you said
"The flowers will bloom again"

[CHORUS — female, letting go, full chest voice, tears falling but strength shining through, emotional and powerful]
FEMALE:
Sakura no chikai — under falling petals I swore
I would carry your light through every closing door
Sakura no chikai — even when the winter's long
I will bloom again, I will be strong
This is my promise, this is my song

[VERSE 2 — male, jumps in low and rough, relates to her pain like it's his own story, raw and unpolished]
MALE:
I left our town with nothing but a bag and broken heart
The city lights were cold but I knew where to start
Every stage I stepped on, I could feel you in the air
Your voice in every melody, your warmth in every prayer

[male, voice rising, emotional, losing control]
When I feel like giving up, when the night won't end
I close my eyes and see your face
[scream] AND RISE UP ONCE AGAIN!

[male fades out — steps back, lets her take it home]

[CHORUS — female, stronger now, empowered by his words, fierce and powerful]
FEMALE:
Sakura no chikai — under falling petals I swore
I would carry your light through every closing door
Sakura no chikai — even when the winter's long
I will bloom again, I will be strong
This is my promise, this is my song

[BRIDGE — female, stripped down, raw and vulnerable, almost whispering like talking to him one last time]
FEMALE:
The cherry trees still bloom every spring
And I still feel you in everything
The ramen shop is gone now, the town has changed its face
But your love is the anchor that time cannot erase

[SPOKEN — female, voice cracks, a tear falls, barely able to speak]
FEMALE: I made it, Dad... I made it...

[FINAL CHORUS — female, scream, all out, raw anger and pain mixed with love, no holding back, giving everything]
FEMALE:
SAKURA NO CHIKAI! [yell] I will never let you down!
I'm wearing your strength like a second crown!
SAKURA NO CHIKAI! [growl] Through the fire and the rain!
[yell] Every time I fall, I'll rise again!
[yell] This is my promise!
[scream] This is my fight!
Carrying your light into the endless night!

[OUTRO — female, exhausted and gentle, like the storm passed, a sad smile, voice fading]
FEMALE:
Mm-mm, mm-mm...
The petals fall, but I stand tall
Mm-mm... I remember spring...
